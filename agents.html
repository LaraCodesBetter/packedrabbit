Questions agents ask

Is this allowed under RESPA?
You're buying a gift for your own client. We never pay you for referrals, and you never pay us for any. If a lender or title company offers to pay for your codes, check with your broker first.

My clients already get a moving service.
Most cover utilities and address changes. This handles the boxes, the furniture, the truck and the movers' quotes. They work fine together.

What if a client doesn't use it?
Codes never expire. Give it to the next one.

Does it work on Android?
Yes — it installs from any phone's browser, with store versions on the way.
