A mover offering a truck a day or two early isn't always doing you a favor. If you're not packed, you pay them to pack. Keep your date, in writing.Each quote earns stars from facts you can check, and loses one for every pressure trick.On a move between states, a mover can't demand more than 110% of a non-binding estimate to hand over your things.Keep every box under about 50 pounds, or under 40 if you're lifting it yourself. Heavy things go in small boxes.Enter the cubic feet you booked with your mover and the truck drawing measures against that.Boxes that ride with you, like Open First and medicine, aren't in the count for the movers.
