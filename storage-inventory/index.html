Where did we put the winter coats?

Bins in the garage, boxes in the attic, a storage unit across town. A storage inventory tells you which bin and where it is, before you climb the ladder.

Make a storage inventory in an afternoon

  1. Give every bin a number. GAR-01 for the garage, ATT-01 for the attic, BSMT-01 for the basement, UNIT-01 for a storage unit. Put the number on the end that faces out.
  2. One photo and a few words. "Winter coats, boots, the blue scarf." Words you'd search for, not a full list.
  3. Say where it's kept. Garage, back shelf, top. Storage unit, left wall. That's the part people skip, and the part that saves the trip.
  4. Update the bin when you swap. When the summer shoes come out and the winter coats go in, change that bin's words. Don't start a new list.

What's worth keeping track of

  • Seasonal clothes: winter coats, summer shoes, snow boots.
  • Holiday decorations, so next December starts with the right bin.
  • Kids' art and school papers you're keeping.
  • Hand-me-downs waiting for the next size.
  • Important papers, and where the originals are.
  • Camping gear, sports equipment and the tools you use twice a year.

Storage unit, garage and basement tips

  • Put what you'll want most often near the door, and leave a walkway to the back.
  • Take a photo of the whole unit or shelf once it's packed, so you can see the layout from home.
  • Keep bins off the floor in a damp basement or garage, and keep paper and fabric out of the dampest spots.
  • Clear bins show what's inside; numbered labels still matter, because you search the number, not the color.

Questions people ask

How do I keep track of what's in my storage bins?

Number each bin, take one photo of what's inside, write a few words you'd search for, and note where the bin is kept. Update that bin's words whenever you swap what's in it.

How do I organize a storage unit so I can find things?

Put the things you'll need most near the door, leave a walkway, number every box, and keep a list that says which box holds what and where it sits.

What should I label storage bins with?

A number with a short place code, such as GAR-01 or UNIT-04, on the end that faces out. The number is what you look up; the contents live in your list.

Can I use the same list for the garage, the attic and a storage unit?

Yes. One list with a place for each bin means one search finds anything, wherever it's kept.
