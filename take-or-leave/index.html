Before you pay to move anything valuable

On a move between states, basic coverage (released value) pays 60 cents a pound, not what a piece is worth. For anything you'd hate to lose, ask the price of Full Value Protection, in writing. Source: FMCSA, Your Rights and Responsibilities When You Move.
