The rules a helper follows

  1. Every box goes in the room written on its label.
  2. Heavy things go in small boxes. Over about 50 pounds is too heavy.
  3. Never stack anything on a box marked do-not-stack.
  4. Stack boxes one deep with the label facing out, and leave a walkway between rows, so every label can be read.
  5. Medicine, papers, pets and anything marked "goes with me" never go on the truck.
  6. Ask before moving any medical or mobility equipment, and never unplug it.
  7. Keep every walkway, doorway and wheelchair path clear.

Privacy comes with it

Each person's list stays on their own device. They choose which helper can ask, a helper can look but not change anything unless they allow it, and medical, documents, insurance and money are never shared, whatever is allowed.
