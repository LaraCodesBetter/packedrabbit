Use it right here. No App Store.

The Packed Rabbit runs in your browser. Nothing to download, no account, no password. Put it on your home screen and it opens like any other app, even with no signal.

Open The Packed Rabbit

Your list stays on your phone.

On iPhone or iPad

  1. Open this page in Safari, then tap Open The Packed Rabbit.
  2. Tap the Share button: the square with an arrow pointing up.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add. The rabbit is on your home screen.

On Android

  1. Open this page in Chrome, then tap Open The Packed Rabbit.
  2. Tap the three dots at the top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app, then Install.

On a computer

  1. Open Open The Packed Rabbit in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar.
  3. Click Install. It opens in its own window. In Safari on a Mac, use File, then Add to Dock.

Good to know

  • It's the whole app, with nothing held back for a download.
  • Works offline. After the first visit it opens with no signal, in a basement or a moving truck.
  • Your list stays on your device. No account, nothing about your move leaves your phone. Make a backup file from the app and keep a copy somewhere safe.
  • The countdown on your home screen. Once it's on your home screen, turn on "Days left on the app icon" in Move details and the rabbit shows how many days are left.
  • Updates itself. Close it and reopen it to get the newest version.
  • Price: the first three boxes come before you pay. Then the app is $9.99 a month until you cancel, with a reminder before every charge.
  • Questions? Write to hello@packedrabbit.com. We answer within two business days.
