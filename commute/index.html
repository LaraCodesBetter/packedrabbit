How this works

Trips. Your days a week, times 50 working weeks, divided by 12, is how many days a month you go in. Each day is there and back, so the miles and minutes count twice.

What a mile costs. The IRS sets standard mileage rates every year. In 2026 the business rate is 72.5 cents a mile, based on an annual study of the fixed and variable costs of running a car: gas and upkeep, but also insurance and the car losing value. The medical and moving rate, 20.5 cents, is based on the variable costs only. If your insurance and payment stay the same either way, the smaller number is closer to what changes. If you know your own cost a mile, use that.

Your time. The hours are shown on their own. They're only turned into dollars if you type what an hour is worth to you.

What it leaves out. Utilities, insurance and taxes can differ between the two places, and so can a second car you might not need. Add what you know to the rent. These are estimates, rounded, not promises.

Comparing two cities, not two neighborhoods? See what pay matches yours in another state, how much money you need to move in or what breaking your lease could cost.

Source: Internal Revenue Service, IRS sets 2026 business standard mileage rate at 72.5 cents per mile, up 2.5 cents (IR-2025-128, December 29, 2025). The 50 working weeks a year is our own round number. Checked November 2026.
