What the app is not

It is a tool for organizing a move. It is not legal, financial, insurance or medical advice, and nobody here is a lawyer, broker or doctor.

It helps you check a mover's licensing, read a quote, understand a bill of lading and keep your claim deadlines, using publicly available federal guidance. It does not vet movers, and we are not party to any contract between you and one.

Measurements, cubic feet, weights and truck sizes are estimates calculated from what you type in. They are a good deal better than guessing. They are not a survey, and a mover's own estimate is the one you are contractually dealing with.
