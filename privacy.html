Privacy

Last updated 26 November 2026.

The short version. The Packed Rabbit needs no account. Your move (your boxes, photos, lists and notes) is kept on your own device. We do not receive it, so we cannot read it, sell it, lose it or be made to hand it over. A few things you choose to do ask another service for help, such as an address search or dictation in Chrome, and each is explained below. What we do receive is small: your email address if you ask for email updates or to test the app, and a payment record if you buy on this website.

What we collect

Nothing about your move, ever. The app does not create an account and does not send your move to us: not your boxes, photos, medicines, documents or lists. The one exception is a photo you ask the rabbit to read, below, and that passes straight through and isn't kept.

Visits to this website, counted, not tracked. We count visits to packedrabbit.com with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which our website host provides. It uses no cookies, doesn't follow you to other sites and doesn't identify you. We see totals: how many visits, which pages, which country, and which site sent the visit. We use these counts only to improve The Packed Rabbit. We never sell them or share them for anyone's advertising.

Lookups you start. A few buttons ask another service a question on your behalf, and only when you tap them. Searching for an address sends what you type to OpenStreetMap's address search. Looking up a vehicle sends its VIN to the U.S. National Highway Traffic Safety Administration. Buttons marked with an arrow, such as "Find its listing photos", open another website in your browser. None of these send your boxes, photos or lists anywhere.

In the Android app. The app itself sends nothing anywhere. Your boxes, photos, lists and papers stay in its private storage on your phone, and a backup is a file you choose where to save. The microphone button hands what you say to your phone's own speech service, which turns it into text under its maker's settings. Paying goes through Google Play's billing, which uses the internet under Google's own policies.

In the iPhone app. The app itself sends nothing anywhere. Your boxes, photos, lists and papers stay in its private storage on your iPhone, and a backup is a file you choose where to save. The microphone button uses Apple's speech recognition on the phone itself, so what you say doesn't leave it. Paying goes through Apple's own subscriptions, which use the internet under Apple's own policies.

Only if you choose:

  • Email updates. If you type your email address and check "Yes, email me", we receive that address, the date, and where in the app you signed up. We use it to send moving tips and news about The Packed Rabbit. We never sell it or share it for anyone else's marketing. Every email has a link to stop them, and "Stop the emails" in the app removes your address from our list.
  • Reading a photo. Members can tap "Read this photo" on a box, a piece of furniture, a mover's quote or a listing, and the app asks once whether that's all right. Only that one photo goes to our server, which passes it to Claude, the AI made by Anthropic, to read, and returns what it read to your phone. We keep neither the photo nor the answer. Our server counts how many photos each phone sent that day, under a random number made on your phone, so no one can run up the bill; it doesn't know who you are. Anthropic handles the photo under its commercial terms: it doesn't train its AI on it, and it may hold it for a short time for safety checks before deleting it. Nothing the rabbit reads is saved until you tap Use this, and it never replaces what you typed. Saying "No thanks" turns it off on that phone.
  • Buying the full app. Payment is handled by Stripe, which receives your card details and email address under its own privacy policy. We receive your email address and a record of the payment, and we email you an unlock code for it. We never see your card number. The monthly plan is billed by Stripe, and you can cancel it anytime.
  • Buying in the iPhone or Android app. Payment is handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies. The app asks Apple or Google, on your phone, whether your plan is active, and unlocks if it is. We see the sale in Apple's or Google's own reports, without your card or account details. You manage or cancel the plan in your Apple or Google account settings.

Where your information lives

Inside your own browser's storage on the device you installed it on, including your box inventory and photographs, furniture and measurements, dates and appointments, medications, doctors and medical notes, documents, insurance, budget and account details, pets, and the people helping you.

In the iPhone and Android apps, the same information is kept in the app's own private storage on that phone instead. It never leaves the phone.

Deleting the app from your device deletes this. We have no copy to restore it from, which is why the app asks you to make your own backups.

Account numbers

The app never asks for a bank account or card number. If you type an insurance policy number or a travel member number, it stays on this phone and is never included when you share your move. It is in the backup file you make, so keep that file somewhere private.

Sharing a move

You can send your move to someone else as an encrypted file, or share it between your own devices. When you do:

  • It is encrypted on your device before it leaves, using AES-GCM with a key derived from your passphrase. Whoever is carrying the file — including any server you choose to use — cannot read it.
  • Certain categories never leave your device, whatever you select: medical information, insurance, powers of attorney, money, documents, payment cards, and the go-bag.
  • If you use the optional sync feature, the address of that server is one you type in. We do not run one and there is no default.

Dictation

If you use the microphone button, your device's browser turns speech into text. On Safari this happens on the device. On Chrome and Edge the audio is sent to the browser's maker to be transcribed, exactly as your keyboard's microphone key has always done. Every field it fills can be typed instead, if you'd rather nothing is sent.

Links out

The app links to government and company websites — DMV offices, utility providers, the FMCSA mover lookup. Opening one takes you to that organization's own site, under their privacy policy, not ours. We are not told that you followed a link.

Payment

If you buy the full app on this website, Stripe handles the payment on its own systems. Stripe receives your card details and email address under its own privacy policy. We receive your email address and a record of the payment, and we use them only to send your unlock code and your receipts. Card details never reach us or the app.

If you buy it inside the iPhone or Android app, Apple or Google handles the payment. We receive no payment details; the app checks the purchase on your phone.

Gift codes from a business

A real estate agent, a leasing office or an employer can buy codes in bulk and give them to the people they help move. Opening a code doesn't send anything to us or to the business: the business never sees your name, your boxes, your photos, your lists or anything else in your move.

When you pay on our website, Stripe sends you back to the app and it asks our server whether that payment went through. We keep only the payment's reference number and the unlock code it was given, so opening the page again shows the same code. Nothing about you or your move.

Businesses we write to

We keep a list of businesses that serve people who move (property managers, agents, move managers and the like): the business name, its public website and the work email it publishes, and our emails with them. We use it only to write to them about The Packed Rabbit. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and anyone who unsubscribes is never written to again.

Testing the app before it reaches the app stores

If you write to hello@packedrabbit.com to test the app, we keep your email address and what you tell us in that email (when you're moving, what kind of phone you have). We use it only to send your tester code and one short note a week, and, on Android, to add the Google account you give us to Google Play's test list, which Google then holds under its own privacy policy. Feedback you send is read to fix the app. We never sell or share any of it, we don't track what you do in the app, and we delete it whenever you ask.

Children

The app is not directed at children and sends us nothing about anyone's move. The helper feature lets a child pack a room on a shared device without seeing the rest of the move; that information stays on the device like everything else.

Your rights

Laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to see, correct, export and delete the personal information a company holds about you. We hold very little: if you bought on this website, your email address and the payment record; if you asked for email updates, your email address; if you signed up to test the app, your email and what you told us. To see or delete any of it, email hello@packedrabbit.com. Everything about your move is already in your hands: the app can export the whole of it, and deleting the app deletes all of it.

Changes

If this ever changes — if a version of the app does send something to us — it will say so on this page with a new date, and the app will ask you first.
