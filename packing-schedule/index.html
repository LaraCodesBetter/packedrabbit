    How this works

    Your rooms give a box count, the same way as the boxes calculator. That count is divided by the boxes you pack on a packing day to get the number of packing days. Those days are counted back from the day before the move, on the days of the week you pack, with one spare day added for whatever runs over. The day before the move is kept for the last things.

    Rooms go in order from least used to most used, so the home keeps working while you pack:

    1. Garage and storageThings you won't need before the move
    2. OfficeBooks and files, in small boxes
    3. DiningServing dishes, table linens, decor
    4. Living roomBooks, decor, games. The TV goes last
    5. BedroomsOut-of-season clothes first. Keep a week's worth out
    6. BathroomsSpares first. Daily things go in the first-night box
    7. KitchenRarely used things first. Keep a plate, cup and pan each

    Pack heavy things such as books, tins and tools in small boxes and light, bulky things in large ones, and keep every box light enough to lift without straining. Keep medicine, documents, chargers and keys with you, not in a box.

    Every number here is an estimate. Box counts per room, the 6-a-day starting pace, the spare day and the room order are our own suggestions, not a published standard. If you are short on time, adding a packing day a week usually helps more than packing more boxes a day.

    Sources: packing rules (light items in large boxes, heavy items in small boxes, no more than 30 to 40 pounds a carton) from State University of New York at Fredonia, Facilities Services moving guide.
