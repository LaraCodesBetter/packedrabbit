Questions people ask first

How do I check a moving quote for hidden fees?

Paste the quote's text into the checker at the top of this page. It flags paperwork fees like a "binding estimate fee", a deposit that's too big, the weakest coverage already ticked, a missing USDOT number, pressure to book today, and signs you're dealing with a broker instead of a mover. It runs in your browser and nothing you paste is sent anywhere.

Is it really free?

Yes. During early access the whole app is free, with no card and no account. Unlimited boxes, photos, labels and the quote checker stay free for good. When paid plans open, the full app is $39 once, with nothing that renews, and you get 7 free days first.

How do you make money if it's free?

From the one-time price of the full app, and from plans for businesses that help people move. Never from movers: we don't take a commission or a referral fee, and we never sell anyone's number.

Will moving companies call me?

No. We never ask for your phone number, and there's nothing to sell to a mover. That's the whole point.

Do I need an account?

No. Open it and start. Everything you enter stays on your own phone.

How do I put it on my phone?

iPhone and iPad: open the app in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: open it in Chrome and tap Install. On a computer it just runs in the browser. Once it is on your home screen it works with the wifi off.

Who made this?

Someone who was moving, not a moving company. Every warning sign on this site came from a real move this fall.

Where do the answers come from?

Federal and consumer sources: the FMCSA, the FTC and the CFPB, plus state law where it matters. Each answer names its source.
