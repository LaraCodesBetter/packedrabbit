Moving questions, answered

How far ahead should I book movers?

Four to eight weeks is normal for a local move. For a move between states, or any move from May to September or at the end of a month, aim for eight to twelve weeks, because those dates fill first. Read more

When should I start packing?

Four to six weeks out, starting with what you won't miss: books, out-of-season clothes, decorations and spare bedding. Finish one room before starting the next. Kitchen, bathroom and bedding go last, in the final two days. Read more

How many boxes do I need?

A rough start: a studio needs 15 to 25 boxes, one bedroom 25 to 40, two bedrooms 40 to 60, and three bedrooms 60 to 90. Add more for lots of books or kitchenware. Use mostly small and medium boxes, and put heavy things in small ones. Read more

What size moving truck do I need?

Add up the cubic feet of your boxes and furniture, then add about 15% for packing room. As a guide, a 10-foot truck holds about 400 cubic feet, a 15-foot about 760, a 20-foot about 1,000 and a 26-foot about 1,700. Read more

Will my sofa fit through the door?

You carry a sofa long end first, so what has to pass the doorway is its two smaller measurements, usually depth and height. If the smaller one is narrower than the door, it goes through straight. If not, try it on its side. Taking the legs off often saves 4 to 6 inches. Read more

How do I check a moving quote for hidden fees?

Add up every line yourself. Look for paperwork fees such as a "binding estimate fee" or "booking fee", stairs and long-carry charges, a large deposit, and pressure to book today. The free quote checker on this site flags them for you, and nothing you paste is sent anywhere. Read more

How do I know a mover is legitimate?

A mover crossing state lines must have a USDOT number. Look it up on the federal FMCSA site and check that it's active and matches the company's name. Then ask directly whether they are the mover or a broker who sells the job to someone else. Read more

What won't movers take?

Anything flammable, explosive or corrosive, such as propane tanks, gasoline, paint, aerosols and pool chemicals. Most won't take perishable food or plants on a long move. Medicines, documents, cash, jewelry and keys should travel with you anyway. Read more

How do I keep pets safe on moving day?

Keep them in a closed, quiet room or with a friend while the doors are open. Open doors and strangers are how pets get lost. Update the microchip and their tags with your new address before you go. Read more

What if the mover won't unload until I pay more?

On a move between states, federal rules cap what a mover can demand at delivery: 110% of a non-binding estimate, or the binding price plus services you agreed to. Anything more must be billed later. Offer the allowed amount in writing. Read more

How long do I have to file a damage claim?

On a move between states, you have nine months from delivery to file a written claim. The mover must acknowledge it within 30 days and pay, refuse or make a firm offer within 120 days. Read more

How do I get my security deposit back?

Do exactly what the move-out section of your lease says, then photograph every empty, clean room with the date. Deadlines depend on the state, for example 30 days in New Jersey and 45 days in Virginia, and the landlord must itemize any deductions. Read more
