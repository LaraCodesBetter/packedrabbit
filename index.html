Movers

It reads the quote before you sign it.

Paste the estimate or photograph it. It checks the company's federal registration, says whether you're dealing with the mover or a broker, and points out the lines that grow on moving day.

Then it holds on to the paperwork that matters: the bill of lading, the inventory, the valuation you chose, the claims deadline. It won't call the move finished while a box is still missing.
