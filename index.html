What it does that the others don't

Every moving app hands you an empty box list and wishes you luck. Not one of them asks what kind of move this is.

It knows whether things fit

Doorway widths checked against real furniture dimensions. Floor coverage, turning space, and a printable sheet for the crew. Everybody has a sofa that won't go round the corner.

It plans around the day you're having

Red, yellow, green. On a red day it shows you one thing instead of nine, and nothing is ever marked late. Everybody has bad days in the middle of a move.

It gets your deposit back

Fourteen move-out obligations, your lease wording captured word for word, cleaning receipts and the signed walkthrough. Usually worth more than everything else here combined.

It makes quotes comparable

One packet with real cubic feet, stairs, carry distance and parking — sent to every mover in the same format. Then it flags the quote that's too low to be true.

It moves your care with you

Pharmacy transfers, records requests, and the Medicare Advantage window that quietly closes two months after you move. Everybody has a doctor.

It handles the garage

Boats, cars, trailers, mowers, pianos, gun safes — with VIN decoding from the federal database and title paperwork tracked. Nobody else covers any of it.

Your move, not a database

There is no account and no server. Everything you enter — every box, every photograph, your medications, your money — stays on your phone. We can't see it. Nobody can.

Share a copy with whoever's helping and it's locked before it leaves the device, with a passphrase only you know. Seven things never go at all, whatever you tick: doctors and medicine, insurance, powers of attorney, money, documents, cards and your go-bag.

The trade, honestly: your phone is the only copy. The app nags you weekly to back it up, and backing up is free.

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. There's nothing to sign up for and no password. Open it and start.

Does it work without signal?

Yes. Add it to your home screen once while online, and everything after that works with no connection — a lift, a plane, a house with the power already off.

Can two people share one move?

Yes, two ways. Send a copy by message, or share a login — a code and a passphrase. Either way it merges rather than overwriting, and it's encrypted before it leaves your phone.

What happens if I lose my phone?

You lose the move, unless you've backed it up. That's the honest cost of having no server. The app reminds you weekly, and backing up takes one tap and is free.

Is this only for a certain kind of move?

No. It's built to the highest standard we could manage, which happens to make it work well whether you're a student with four boxes or a family with a house, a workshop and a boat.

Can I get a refund?

Thirty days, no questions asked. Email and it's done.
