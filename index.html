Did you know?

Six of the tips inside the app. Share any of them with someone who's moving.

Heavy things go in small boxes. Books in a big box won't lift, and the bottom gives out.

Photograph the inside of a box before you tape it. Then you never open five boxes to find one thing.

Measure a sofa's depth, not its length. It goes through the door on its side.

Pack an Open First box and keep it with you: sheets, chargers, medicine, toilet paper.

On a move between states, you have at least 9 months after delivery to file a written damage claim.

Photograph every room of the old place, empty, before you hand back the keys. It's your proof for the deposit.
