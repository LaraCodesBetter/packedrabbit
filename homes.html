What it's really like to rent there.

Ratings from people who lived there — each one checked against a lease or bill, then the proof is deleted. Repairs, deposits, honesty, privacy.

Ratings open shortly. Lived somewhere worth warning — or praising — people about? Open the app, add the home under Renting with a record, and rate it there.

How ratings work

  • Anonymous. Reviewers' names, unit numbers and (if they choose) dates are never shown. The proof is seen once by the person checking it, then deleted.
  • Only someone who lived there can rate it. They show a lease page, utility bill or bank statement with their name and the address. We check it once, then delete it.
  • We never remove a rating for being critical. We remove one only for personal details, threats, or if the proof doesn't hold up.
  • Owners and managers can reply once to each rating after proving they own or manage the building.
  • We don't rate tenants, and never will publish ratings of people who rent.

Rate a place you lived: open the app → Pack and place → Renting with a record.

Own or manage a building?

Show a deed, property tax bill or management agreement for the address. Once it's checked, you'll get a private reply code by email or text. The proof is then deleted.
