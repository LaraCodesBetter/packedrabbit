How to store Christmas decorations so you can find them next year

Pack them once, carefully, and next December starts with the right bin instead of every bin.

Before anything goes in a box

  • Check each set of lights for broken or cracked sockets, frayed or bare wires and loose connections. Throw out damaged sets instead of storing them.
  • Take the batteries out of anything that runs on them, so nothing leaks over the year.
  • Let anything damp dry first: wreaths, tree skirts, outdoor pieces.
  • Take one photo of the tree and the mantel while they're still up. Next year you'll know what went where.

Pack it so it comes out whole

  • Ornaments go in a box with dividers, each one wrapped in tissue or packing paper, heaviest on the bottom.
  • Wind each string of lights around a piece of cardboard, and write on the card what it goes on: tree, porch, window.
  • Wreaths go in a round box or a bag hung on a hook, so nothing crushes them.
  • An artificial tree goes in its own bag or box, with each section's letter or color marked.
  • Small parts, like hooks and the tree stand's screws, go in a zip bag taped to what they belong to.
  • Candles and anything with wax stay out of the hottest part of the attic, and paper and fabric stay out of a damp basement.

Number the bins and say what's inside

  1. Give each bin a number, HOL-01, HOL-02, on the end that faces out.
  2. One photo and a few words: "Tree lights, white, three sets." "Glass ornaments, Grandma's."
  3. Say where it's kept: garage, top shelf. Attic, by the hatch.
  4. Mark what comes out first: the tree stand and the lights, so you're not unpacking ornaments onto a bare tree.

Questions people ask

What is the best way to store Christmas ornaments?

In a box with dividers, each ornament wrapped in tissue or packing paper, heaviest ones at the bottom, in a spot that stays dry and doesn't get very hot.

How do I store Christmas lights without tangling them?

Check them first and throw out damaged sets, then wind each string around a piece of cardboard and write on the card where it goes.

How do I find my decorations next year?

Number each bin, take one photo of what's inside, write a few words and where it's kept. Next year, search the list instead of opening every bin.

Where should I store holiday decorations?

Somewhere dry and not too hot. Keep candles and wax out of the hottest part of an attic, and paper and fabric out of a damp basement.
