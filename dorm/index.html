Will it fit in my dorm room?

The school gives you a bed, a desk, a dresser and a wardrobe. Find out how much floor that leaves before you buy the futon.

Your room

Your housing office's page for your hall usually lists the room size. 16 by 10 feet is one real example (Woodland Hall at SIUE).

What you're bringing

    Working it out…

    What fits under the bed

    Most school beds adjust. The schools below list 11 to 14 inches under the bed at a low setting and up to about 32 inches at the highest. Leave an inch so the bin slides out.

    Before move-in day

    • Every school we checked uses twin XL beds, 36 by 80 inches. Regular twin sheets come up short; buy twin XL.
    • Talk to your roommate before you shop. One fridge, one rug and one TV, not two of each.
    • Check your hall's own page. Furniture and room sizes change from one hall to the next, even on one campus.
    • Check what's allowed. Your housing office lists the rules on fridges, cords and anything that heats up.
    • Pack in labeled bins, not trash bags. Bins stack, slide under the bed, and come home again in May.

    Furniture sizes from university housing offices, checked November 2026: University of New Hampshire, University of Oregon, Syracuse University, Rowan University and SIUE. This is an estimate of floor space, not a floor plan; doors, radiators and windows take room too.
