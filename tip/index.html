    How this works

    The ranges. These are the commonly cited amounts for each mover: $20 to $50 for a day's work on a local move, $50 to $100 a day on a long-distance move, $4 to $15 an hour, or 10% to 20% of the total bill shared across the crew. "Fine" uses the bottom of the range, "Good" the middle, and "Above and beyond" the top. Customs vary by place and by job, and none of these is a rule.

    Rounding. Each mover's amount is rounded to the nearest $5 (or the nearest dollar under $10) so it is easy to hand over in cash.

    Who gets it. Hand each mover their own tip, so everyone gets a share. If one crew loads and another unloads, each crew gets its own tip. On a job that runs over several days, tip at the end of each day.

    Cash or card. Cash is the most common way and the easiest to split. If you want to tip by card, check or app, ask the company first: a card tip may not reach each mover separately.

    Food and drinks. Kind, not expected, and not part of the tip. Ask about allergies before you order.
