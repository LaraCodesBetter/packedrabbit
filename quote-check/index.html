What it looks for

  • Paperwork fees, like a "binding estimate fee"
  • A deposit that's too big, and money due before your things are even loaded
  • The weakest coverage already checked for you
  • No USDOT number, or signs you're dealing with a broker instead of a mover
  • Pressure to book today, and a pickup window measured in days

Sources: the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration's Protect Your Move and its rules for household goods movers (49 CFR Part 375). This isn't legal advice.
