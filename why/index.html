Why I built this.

Consumers educating consumers. Stop the scams.

I was building an app to keep my whole move in one place. I didn't know it would turn into a warning for other people, about the worst of this industry and the best.

I was quoted by three movers, and only two showed up. One quote wanted 50% up front, then 70% before anyone set foot in my house. I called three cleaning companies for quotes, and the same woman answered all three numbers. I was told two to three hours, and it took nine on each end, for one bedroom. A listing photo showed my exact bedroom set upstairs, and there is no way that bed gets up those stairs.

And I can't remember what's in a box once it's taped. So I photographed every one. I packed over 140 boxes, and I know exactly what's inside each of them. The night before Thanksgiving, with most of my kitchen still packed, I found my sauce pans without opening a single other box.

Every tool here is one I needed. Every part of my move in one place.

Where to report a moving scam

Telling someone helps the next person. For a move between states, file with the federal moving regulator's National Consumer Complaint Database. For a move inside one state, the state regulates movers: contact your state attorney general or consumer protection office. And report it to BBB Scam Tracker, so others can see it.
