How this works

The dates travel inside the link. Everything you type goes after the # in the address, and browsers never send that part to a server. We never see it, and neither does anyone else until you send the link yourself.

Your client decides. Their link opens a page that asks, "Would you like to add these to your calendar?" One tap adds every date to their phone's calendar, with a reminder the day before and who to call. In The Packed Rabbit app, the same link adds them to their appointments.

Dates move. Send again. This page keeps your last client's dates on this device. Change what moved and send the new link: the app shows your client what changed instead of adding a date twice.

Never send wire instructions or account numbers. Your client's page reminds them to confirm any payment by calling a number they already know, and a long account number in a note shows only its last four digits.
