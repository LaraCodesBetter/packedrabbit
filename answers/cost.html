What does a move usually cost?

National ranges for full-service movers in 2026: a local move (up to 100 miles) is roughly $500–1,200 for a studio or one bedroom, $1,200–2,500 for two or three bedrooms, and $2,500–5,000 for four or more.

Around 1,000 miles or more: roughly $2,500–5,500, $4,500–8,500 and $8,000–14,000 for the same sizes.

Stairs, packing, peak season and extra insurance add to it. Getting quotes compares your own quotes to the range for your move.

If one quote is far below the rest, be careful: the classic scam is a low price that grows once your things are on the truck.

Source: Opendoor, How Much Does It Cost to Move (2026) · Checked October 2026
