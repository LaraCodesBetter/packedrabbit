What are the deadlines for a military moving claim?

Give written notice of loss or damage within 180 days of delivery. Note what you can on the delivery paperwork that day.

File the itemized claim in DPS within 12 months of delivery for shipments picked up on or after May 15, 2026, or 9 months for shipments picked up on or before May 14, 2026. A late claim only gets the depreciated value.

The mover must confirm your claim within 15 days, then pay, deny or make an offer within 30 days for claims of $1,000 or less, or 60 days for larger ones. If you can't reach a fair settlement, you can transfer the claim to your military claims office within two years of delivery.

Photos of what was inside each box, taken before the move, are the evidence a claim rests on. Check the dates on your own paperwork; they decide. The Packed Rabbit is not affiliated with the Department of Defense.

Source: DoD Personal Property Activity, The Claims Process (ppa.mil, checked October 2026); DoD Personal Property Activity, claims fact sheet (February 2026); Military OneSource · Checked October 2026
