When do I need a new driver's license after moving states?

Most states give you between 10 and 60 days after you become a resident — it varies, so check your new state's DMV.

Book the appointment early; registration and insurance often wait on the license.

Bring proof of your new address, your old license and your Social Security number or card.

Source: State DMVs — varies · Checked October 2026
