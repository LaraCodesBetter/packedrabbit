When do I need a new driver's license after moving states?

It depends on the state, and the range is wide. California gives you 10 days after you become a resident, Florida 30 and Texas 90.

Check your new state's DMV, and book early because appointments fill up.

For a REAL ID, bring a passport or birth certificate, proof of your Social Security number, and two proofs of your new address. Bring your old license too.

Source: Texas DMV, New to Texas (checked Nov 2026); Florida HSMV, New Resident (checked Nov 2026); California DMV; DHS, REAL ID; 6 CFR 37.11 · Checked November 2026
