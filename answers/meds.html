What should I do about medications during the move?

They travel with you, never on the truck.

Refill early so you have a couple of weeks extra, and keep a written list of every medicine and dose.

Ask your pharmacy to transfer prescriptions to one near the new home before you arrive.

Source: Common practice · Checked November 2026
