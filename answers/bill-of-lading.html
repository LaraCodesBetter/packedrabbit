What is a bill of lading?

It's the contract for your move and the receipt for your things, in one document.

On a move between states, the mover must give it to you to sign at least 3 days before loading. You then have 3 days to cancel without penalty.

Check the price, the pickup and delivery dates, and the valuation you chose. Keep your copy with you, not in a box.

Source: eCFR, 49 CFR 375.505; FMCSA, Protect Your Move glossary · Checked October 2026
