Do I need a permit to park a moving truck on the street?

In many cities, yes, or a permit is the only way to hold a legal spot. Rules, fees and lead times vary a lot by city.

Some cities want a day's notice, others several weeks. Fees can run from tens to hundreds of dollars.

Signs often have to go up 24 to 72 hours ahead. A moving container may need a separate permit.

Look on your city's official .gov site for a moving or temporary no-parking permit, and apply early.

Source: City of Boston, Reserve a parking spot for your moving truck; SF.gov, Get a temporary no-parking sign; Seattle DOT, Temporary No Parking · Checked November 2026
