Are moving box kits and bundle packs worth it?

Sometimes. Compare the price per box, not the price of the kit — and count how many of each size you'll really use. Kits often pad the count with sizes you won't need.

Most people need far more small and medium boxes than large ones; large boxes get overfilled and become too heavy.

Buy a little more than you think, from a store that takes unused boxes back, and keep the receipt. The Supplies screen keeps the receipt and the return date.
