Does my renters or homeowners insurance cover my move?

Usually only partly. Home and renters policies often give limited cover for belongings in transit, and some exclude it.

Your deductible applies to any claim, so a small loss may not be worth claiming.

Call your insurer before moving day. Ask what's covered on the truck, and start the policy at the new address on moving day.

Source: NAIC, Leaving Home: Insurance Considerations for a Move; FMCSA, Understanding Valuation and Insurance Options · Checked October 2026
