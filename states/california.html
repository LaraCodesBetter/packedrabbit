Moving to California: utilities and your first-month checklist

Which company serves your new address depends on where in California it is. Each link below goes to that company's own page for starting service. Start it two to three weeks ahead, from the day you get the keys.

Help with the bills

If deposits or bills are a stretch, California runs energy assistance: official assistance page. Ask the utility about deposit waivers too; many waive the deposit with a good payment letter from your old company.

Problems with a utility

The California Public Utilities Commission regulates utilities in California and takes complaints.

New-resident deadlines in California

  • Driver's license: within 10 days of moving. Official page
  • Car registration and title: within 20 days of moving. Official page
  • Inspection: Smog Check required to register a vehicle in California for the first time. Official page
  • California driver license office
  • Register to vote in California

From the state's own websites, checked October 2026. Deadlines can change; check the official page before you rely on one.

Your first month in a new state

  • USPS: change your address: Start mail forwarding up to 30 days early. The real site is usps.com.
  • Your new state's motor vehicle office: Driver's license, registration and title. Most states set a short deadline after you move, so check yours first.
  • Register to vote: Routes you to the official registration for the new state.
  • Medicaid, if you have it: Medicaid doesn't move with you. You apply again in the new state.
  • Health insurance: A permanent move can open a special enrollment period.
  • Social Security: Change your address once here; Medicare gets it from Social Security.

Utility companies and service areas checked October 2026. Service areas follow addresses, not county lines exactly: confirm with the company before you book.
