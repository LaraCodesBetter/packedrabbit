Dear {greeting},

{opening line}

I'm Lara Cerutti, founder of The Packed Rabbit. I built the app after moving twice in just over a year, both times to a different state. I wasn't even unpacked from the first move when I had to pack up for the second. Every time I needed something, I had to open box after box to find the right one: hours of extra work that an app like this would have saved me.

The Packed Rabbit lets people photograph what goes into each box and find anything later in seconds, from moving day to the last box unpacked. The app also has free tools to check a moving quote and a mover before paying anyone, because moving scams are everywhere.

We launch soon on Android, iPhone and the web. Before we do, I'm giving a sneak peek to a small group of first partners (property managers, agents, move managers, relocation and storage teams) who want to give the people they serve a calmer move. Think of the app as a closing or move-in gift, like a home warranty but for the move itself, for a fraction of the cost.

I'd love for you to be one of the first to see the app. Would you be open to a short call, or should I send a one-page overview?

Warmly,

Lara Cerutti

Founder, The Packed Rabbit
