The rules we keep

  1. We never sell anyone's move. What people put in the app stays on their phone. You get a count of codes used, never what's in anyone's boxes.
  2. No commissions, no referral fees. We don't get paid for sending anyone to anyone, and we don't pay for referrals either.
  3. Paid never changes a verdict. The quote reader checks every mover the same way, including any who list with us.
  4. Nothing to integrate. A code goes in an email you already send. Integration is there when a large account wants it, not before.
