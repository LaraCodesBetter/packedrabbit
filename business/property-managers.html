What changes at your properties

  • Move-out photos, dated, every room. The app walks residents through photographing each room before they hand back the keys. Disputes get shorter when both sides have pictures.
  • Hallways stay clear on move day. Furniture is checked against your doorways and elevator before the truck arrives, so the sofa that won't fit is found on Tuesday, not on Saturday in the stairwell.
  • Elevator and loading dock bookings land on their calendar, with your rules, so the reservation isn't forgotten.
  • Utilities and address changes on one list, with the move-in date already set.

What residents get

  • Every box numbered, labeled with a QR code, photographed, and findable by typing or saying what's in it.
  • Furniture checked against every doorway, stairway and elevator before the truck comes.
  • The truck size worked out from what was actually packed, and movers' quotes read for brokers and hidden costs.
  • Move day run from one screen: what goes on the truck, what rides with them, what arrived.
  • Before-and-after photos of every room, kept with the date, for getting the deposit back.

What you never get

Their data. Photos and inventories stay on the resident's phone; they share them with you only if they choose. That's why residents trust it, and why it counts as fair evidence when they do.

Each code is a full year of the app for one household, paid once. A year on the monthly plan is $119.88, so even a single code saves $8.99. The year starts when your client opens it, not when you buy it. Codes never expire and carry no fees, and if a bigger order would cost less, you pay the lower total. One price list for every buyer. Each one is a personal link with your name and welcome note built in: put it in an email, a text, or a QR code on a card. Larger volumes are priced by conversation.

Portfolio pricing per unit is available for managers with more than 1,000 units. We don't add it to residents' fees, and we'd rather you didn't either.
