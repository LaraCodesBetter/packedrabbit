PCS season, organized.

Whether the government's movers pack you or you move yourself, the same things go wrong: a box that never arrives, a dresser that comes back broken, a receipt you can't find when it's time to get paid. This keeps the record that fixes all three.

Moving yourself (PPM)

  • Weight tickets in one place: the empty and full weigh-ins, with photos of each ticket.
  • Every receipt kept as you go: fuel, tolls, the truck, packing supplies.
  • The truck size from what you packed, so you rent the right one the first time.

When the movers pack you

  • Your own inventory, with photos, taken before the crew arrives. When their inventory says "box, misc.", yours says what was in it.
  • Damage photographed at delivery, dated, next to the before photo.
  • The claim deadline on your calendar, so it doesn't pass while you're still unpacking.

For family programs and sponsors

A code unlocks the whole app on a family's phone, and everyone helping them move uses it free. Family nonprofits, unit family-support groups and local businesses can buy codes at our lowest rate, $15 a family, and hand them out before PCS season.
