For relocation and HR

The lump sum goes further when someone checks the quote.

More relocations are paid as a lump sum, so more employees are managing a mover, a truck and a deadline on their own. Give them the app that does it with them, for less than the cost of one mistake.

Talk to us

What it does for a transferee

  • Every box numbered, labeled with a QR code, photographed, and findable by typing or saying what's in it.
  • Furniture checked against every doorway, stairway and elevator before the truck comes.
  • The truck size worked out from what was actually packed, and movers' quotes read for brokers and hidden costs.
  • Move day run from one screen: what goes on the truck, what rides with them, what arrived.
  • Before-and-after photos of every room, kept with the date, for getting the deposit back.

And it checks the mover: whether the company on the quote is registered with the federal motor carrier agency, whether it's a broker rather than a mover, and which charges tend to grow on moving day.

Why HR and mobility teams like it

  • No employee data comes to us or to you. Everything stays on the employee's phone, so there's no new system holding personal information.
  • Nothing to integrate. A code goes in the relocation packet or the offer email.
  • A usage count, not surveillance. You see how many codes were opened.
  • For relocation companies: add it to a lump-sum or core tier, per transferee. We don't take part in referral fees in any direction.
